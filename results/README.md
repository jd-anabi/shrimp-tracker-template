# Results

- `REPORT.md`: the template for your report. Copy it to `results/<your name>/REPORT.md` (first name, lowercase)
  and fill in your copy; leave this one as it is. Hand it in by uploading your whole folder `results/<your name>/`
  (not just REPORT.md: `figures/` must sit next to it) to your group's folder in the shared Google Drive.
- `<your name>/figures/`: your figures. The `shrimp.compare` commands save theirs here (`tools_A.png`,
  `ages_edgetam.png`, ...); add others (e.g. a photo of your setup) yourself.
- `work/`: anything big or temporary. Ignored by git.
